# Art provenance and reuse / Происхождение и использование изображений

The 34 PNGs in `assets/` and `variants/` were generated or edited with AI assistance during the Remains Legendary development conversation. `catalog.json` identifies their purpose, prototype status and hashes. Identical copies formerly kept in Assets, Concepts and the 20-weapon concept folder were deduplicated. The two superseded Aurora designs are retained as variants.

The project contributor welcomes study, reuse, modification and continuation of these project-created materials **to the extent they can grant the relevant rights**. Please credit Remains Legendary, disclose AI assistance when redistributing the concepts, and retain this notice. No exclusive ownership, unrestricted commercial clearance or ownership of underlying third-party designs is claimed.

Important scope limitations:

- Pony characters, Fallout Equestria / Fallout / My Little Pony settings and Remains-related imagery may incorporate third-party intellectual property.
- Excalibur was specifically requested with reference to its depiction in **Fate**. The project does not grant rights to that design or franchise.
- Vigilance, the Stable 99 suit and Aurora armor were developed with user-supplied visual references. The provenance/permission for underlying reference designs was not established as a transferable license. Their project-generated interpretations remain reference-derived fan concepts.
- The source reference pictures uploaded by the user and extracted original-game sprites are **not included** here.
- Concept art is not a complete rig, animation sheet or production-ready set of game sprites. Armor rendering in the prototype is a simplified adaptation.

Before redistributing individual files in another project, consider the licenses and third-party rights applicable to those files. This notice does not purport to settle those rights.
